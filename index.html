Questions

Common questions, answered

Can't find your answer here? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on what you need. A simple business website costs far less than custom billing software. After a short conversation about your requirements, we give you a clear estimate before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. As an example, we delivered DC Moto's complete billing and inventory system in 7 days, from understanding their requirements to installing it on their computer. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.

I'm not technical. Will I be able to use it?

Yes. We design everything to be simple, we set it up for you, and we walk you and your team through how to use it. You'll also get clear documentation to refer back to.

Can I ask for changes during the project?

Of course. We share progress along the way and refine things based on your feedback, so the final result fits how you actually work.

Do I have to pay monthly subscription fees?

Not for software we build for you. Unlike off-the-shelf tools, custom software doesn't require a monthly subscription to keep using it. Hosting or optional support plans, if you need them, are discussed upfront.

What happens after the project is delivered?

We stay available for support, fixes, updates and improvements, so your website or software keeps running smoothly as your business grows.
